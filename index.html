Learn with simple examples

Open one lesson. Each one teaches one useful habit.

“My phone broke. Send money to this new account. Don’t call.”
Try this: Pause. Contact the person using a phone number you already know.
A dramatic photo is shared with no date, place, or source.
Try this: Look for the original source before you share it.

Check something suspicious

You do not need to know the answer. “I’m not sure” is always okay.

Good next step: verify before acting or sharing.

CircleCheck does not give a “fake score.” Use these simple next steps:

  • Pause before clicking, paying, or sharing.
  • Find an official or independent source yourself.
  • Ask someone you trust if you are still unsure.
